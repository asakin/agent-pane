# Code of conduct

Agent Pane welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, make feedback specific and constructive, and focus disagreement on ideas and behavior.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable. These expectations apply to this project's issues, pull requests, and other community spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Ariel Sakin, at [asakin@gmail.com](mailto:asakin@gmail.com). Include links and relevant context, and avoid sharing private information beyond what is needed to investigate. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and may include a warning or a temporary or permanent ban.
